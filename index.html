NeuralChess

↗

I built a chess engine to explore how a model can learn through self-play. It brings together a neural network, game-tree search, and a browser interface for playing against the model.

What’s under the hood

The project includes a chess rules engine, automatic differentiation, a NumPy neural network, and Monte Carlo tree search.

QuantPulse

↗

A paper-trading platform for understanding what sits behind a brokerage dashboard. I worked on the React interface, live quote updates, and consistent order state. All trades are simulated.

What’s under the hood

Market and limit orders, portfolio analytics, and strategy backtesting, supported by Node.js, SQLite persistence, and WebSocket updates.
